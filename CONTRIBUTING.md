# Contributing

This list covers resources that help candidates discover jobs, assess opportunities, prepare applications, manage the process, and follow up. It can include tools, data sources, standards, and practical guides. AI is welcome where it helps, but is not a requirement.

## Suggest an entry

Open a pull request with one entry in the most relevant section, or open an issue if you are unsure where it belongs. For each suggestion, include:

- A working link and a concise description of what it does and who it helps.
- Why you would recommend it from your own use or careful evaluation.
- Any relevant limits, such as pricing, geographic coverage, account requirements, or self-hosting needs.
- Your relationship to the project, if you make it, work on it, or benefit from its inclusion.

Prefer resources that are usable, documented, and maintained. Keep descriptions factual rather than promotional. We may decline duplicates, abandoned projects, generic link dumps, or resources that do not directly support a candidate's job hunt. Paid and closed-source projects are eligible when their value is clear.

Use this format and end descriptions with a period:

```md
- [Project](https://example.com) - What it does and why it is useful.
```

If the relevant section does not exist yet, propose one with your entry. Keep sections focused on the actual resources in the list; we will add more categories as the list grows.

## Corrections

Please open an issue or pull request for broken links, inaccurate descriptions, or projects that no longer meet the criteria.
